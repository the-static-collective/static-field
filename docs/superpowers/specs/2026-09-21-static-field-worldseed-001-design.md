# STATIC FIELD // WORLDSEED-001 — Design Constitution

**Status:** design constitution / review gate  
**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Repository:** `the-static-collective/static-field`  
**First playable story:** `THE FIRST BELL`

> **Music does not invent the world. Music makes certain relations in the world audible.**

## 0. Intent

STATIC FIELD is the first playable world in THE CROSSING: a shared human/model worldline in which distinct worlds retain their own laws while people, cards, relationships, memories, receipts, open Threads, and consequences can lawfully travel between them.

STATIC FIELD is not a generic fantasy setting and not a lore wrapper around existing repositories.

It is a **porous double-world**:

1. the ordinary world remains ordinary and attributable;
2. accumulated musical, relational, remembered, and inherited resonance may become navigable without rewriting the underlying occurrence.

The world should feel **post-Midwestern ecclesiastical dadcore**:

- porches;
- garages;
- church basements;
- fellowship halls;
- folding tables and chairs;
- county roads;
- old mixers;
- milk crates;
- chipped cups;
- extension cords;
- repaired handrails;
- kitchens;
- dog bowls;
- parks;
- community rooms;
- amps with electrical tape;
- bulletins, flyers, receipts, cables, and ordinary objects that become sacred through repeated use.

The visual and narrative law is:

> **The holier something becomes, the less expensive it should look.**

This is not because poverty is sacred. It is because meaning in STATIC FIELD accumulates through **attendance, use, repair, relationship, inheritance, witness, and return**, not prestige rendering.

## 1. Story inspirations

STATIC FIELD's meta-story grammar draws from four deliberately strange reference points without copying their worlds, characters, presentation, or trade dress.

### Quantum Leap

Arrival happens inside a story already underway.

The party does not own the world, begin at its beginning, or automatically understand what matters. Existing people, obligations, absences, relationships, and unresolved situations precede arrival.

### Doctor Who

The traveling party supplies continuity more strongly than geography.

Locations, tone, mechanics, point characters, model sessions, and eras may change radically while carried relationships, remembered consequences, cards, open Threads, and questions persist.

### Kingdom Hearts II

Local worlds remain locally meaningful while a deeper pattern slowly earns its way into the foreground.

The meta-story appears first as residue, recurrence, crossings, incompatible ancestry, and shared particulars rather than a giant exposition dump.

### GTA IV

One occurrence may be encountered through multiple protagonists and local charts.

No playable perspective automatically becomes the complete story. Separate storylines can collide around the same event without retroactively absorbing each other.

## 2. THE CROSSING meta-law

The initial mystery is not "an evil multiverse is collapsing."

It is:

> **Things that should be separate are leaving traces in one another.**

Possible symptoms include:

- a song remembering a place where it has not been performed;
- a physical card opening a Gate in a world that does not recognize its origin;
- two unrelated storylines containing the same particular;
- correspondence returning into a context that has materially changed;
- a dream recurring alongside an attributable external occurrence;
- a model session finding a relationship whose later physical consequence was not part of its input;
- an object appearing in distinct histories with unresolved ancestry.

The long reveal is not necessarily that the worlds are breaking.

A central hypothesis of THE CROSSING is:

> **The worlds may be learning how to compose.**

The thematic conflict then becomes:

> **How do we cross well without absorbing one another?**

Not everything should cross.
Not every Gate should open.
Knowledge may cross where authority may not.
A gift may cross where ownership does not.
A memory may be revisited where history may not be rewritten.
A world may receive another world's artifact without inheriting its meaning.

## 3. STATIC FIELD founding proposition

STATIC FIELD is:

> **the ordinary world encountered through accumulated musical resonance.**

The world always preserves two simultaneously valid maps.

### 3.1 Surface Map

Contains constituted, locally attributable particulars:

- people;
- rooms;
- physical objects;
- performances;
- instruments;
- cards;
- local places;
- food;
- roads;
- events;
- actual arrivals and departures;
- receipts;
- explicit witness relationships.

### 3.2 Resonance Map

Contains navigable relations that may emerge from or connect Surface particulars:

- songs;
- remembered motifs;
- open Threads;
- relational echoes;
- dream traces;
- unresolved absences;
- ancestral relationships;
- repeated musical associations;
- Gates;
- Fields;
- Hollows;
- questions.

The Resonance Map may become surreal.

It may not rewrite the Surface Map.

A valid puzzle may require realizing:

> **I am standing in the correct place on the wrong map.**

## 4. World laws

### LAW 1 — The Field remembers occurrences, not fantasies about them

A performance receipt may seed a mythic region.

A mythic region may not rewrite who performed.

A dream may reveal a relation.

A dream may not become evidence that an external occurrence happened.

A narrative interpretation may become locally meaningful.

It may not silently become source authority.

### LAW 2 — Sacredness accumulates through attendance

A place may deepen as people repeatedly:

- arrive;
- care;
- repair;
- play;
- pray;
- argue;
- reconcile;
- cook;
- rest;
- witness;
- leave;
- return;
- remember;
- fail to return;
- leave something unfinished.

This accumulated history is **Field Depth**.

```text
PLACE
+ attributable arrivals
+ attributable acts
+ remembered absences
+ repeated care
+ unresolved business
= FIELD DEPTH
```

Field Depth is not moral worth, player level, ownership, popularity, or truth.

### LAW 3 — Repetition reveals relationship; it does not prove meaning

Three performances at the same porch create three attributable relationships.

The game may use that recurrence as navigable material.

It may not claim recurrence proves cosmic significance, intention, prophecy, ownership, or authority.

### LAW 4 — Songs may become geography

A song, album, motif, performance lineage, or sufficiently rich composition may seed a navigable Region.

The Region remains linked to the sources from which it emerged.

Example conceptual regions:

- THE PORCH
- THE HOUSE
- OPEN E RIDGE
- DOG PARK
- EQUINOX WEATHER
- THE ROAD SO FAR
- THE DAUGHTER'S ARC

These names are world possibilities, not all required WORLDSEED-001 content.

### LAW 5 — The Field responds to parties, not generic accounts

Reachable structures can depend on the particular party:

```text
GateCondition =
  cards
+ people
+ relationships
+ prior receipts
+ current place
+ unresolved Threads
+ temporal state
+ local world rules
```

A player does not unlock every structure merely by having a high account level.

### LAW 6 — Silence remains available

Absence must remain representable.

Examples:

- MISSING PART
- UNPLAYED SONG
- EMPTY CHAIR
- UNANSWERED LETTER
- VOICE NOT PRESENT
- UNFINISHED PERFORMANCE
- PERSON EXPECTED BUT NOT PRESENT
- ROOM WHERE SOMETHING DID NOT HAPPEN

The runtime may not automatically fill a Hollow merely because content would be more convenient.

### LAW 7 — Ordinary verbs are the magic system

STATIC FIELD does not require a generic `MAGIC` action.

Its high-voltage verbs are ordinary:

- OPEN
- CARRY
- FIX
- LISTEN
- CALL
- WAIT
- FEED
- PLAY
- ASK
- RETURN
- REST
- PRAY
- BLESS
- FORGIVE
- REFUSE
- SHOW UP
- RECEIVE
- HOLD
- POUR

A supernatural or dreamlike consequence occurs when an ordinary verb lawfully composes across more layers than expected.

### LAW 8 — Prayer and self-care are capacity mechanics, not virtue scores

The local capacity resource is provisionally called **CHARGE**.

Charge may be restored or transformed through:

- REST;
- TEND;
- PRAY;
- PLAY;
- WITNESS;
- KEEP;
- ASK-FOR-HELP;
- FEED / EAT / DRINK where appropriate.

Low capacity is not failure.

A valid state may be:

```text
POUR unavailable

available:
  RECEIVE
  HOLD
  REST
  ASK-FOR-HELP
```

Prayer is an intentional bounded practice that may alter available actions, attention, relationships, or Gates according to declared local rules. It is not a generic spell point generator and does not certify divine endorsement of a player's interpretation.

### LAW 9 — Worlds continue while the party is away

Returning to a place does not reset it.

Inhabitants may:

- remember;
- misremember;
- move;
- age;
- change commitments;
- send correspondence;
- leave;
- die;
- create descendants;
- become Point Characters later;
- resolve things without the party.

Offscreen does not mean inactive.

### LAW 10 — History does not become a save-state rewrite

Flashbacks, memories, dreams, and replay may expose new information or interpretation.

They cannot edit the receipt of what occurred.

A later understanding becomes a new relation or occurrence.

## 5. Parish topology

A **Parish** is not a denomination, state, ownership boundary, or universal hierarchy.

It is:

> **a recurring ecology of people and places that care for some overlapping set of things.**

Examples:

- a band;
- a family;
- a block;
- a church;
- a festival crew;
- a recovery group;
- a kitchen;
- a Coderoom;
- a few people plus their garages and group chat.

Parishes overlap.

One participant may belong to several.

Membership does not grant authority outside the relationship and context actually established.

Parish topology gives the Field a relational map without turning geography into territory ownership.

## 6. Ecclesiastical machinery

World machinery should remain tactile, repaired, ordinary, and legible.

### THE FELLOWSHIP TABLE

A physical composition surface.

Cards or other particulars arranged on it may become a parseable PostEmahh'n composition.

The table does not authenticate a false card merely because it was placed there.

### THE OFFERING BOX

A handoff structure for leaving an object, card, message, receipt, or resource for an unknown or later participant.

Leaving something does not automatically surrender authorship, copyright, or every future interest.

### THE BULLETIN BOARD

A community-memory surface for:

- Commissions;
- lost items;
- open Threads;
- field rumors;
- prayer requests;
- show flyers;
- missing cables;
- notices;
- offers;
- unresolved questions.

A posted claim remains a posted claim until separately established.

### THE SOUND DESK

A SIGNAL-routing surface that may eventually project or control bounded Static Live / Band Runtime-adjacent functions.

It never invents missing performers.

### THE BAPTISTRY

A rare THRESHOLD apparatus for declared state crossing.

It is not a generic healing fountain.

Any use must state:
- prior state;
- intended crossing;
- witness/authority requirements;
- resulting receipt;
- non-claims.

### THE NURSERY

A protected zone in which interruption is a first-class world mechanic.

Plans must adapt to needs that actually arise.

Dependency, care, and interruption are not treated as failed productivity.

### THE KITCHEN

A high-value embodied-capacity region.

Food and practical care may convert abstract party state back into bodily capacity.

The Kitchen should integrate naturally with future Nourish/help-slip mechanics without becoming a hunger leaderboard.

### THE BASEMENT

Deprecated, archival, unfashionable, half-broken, or superseded things may remain playable here when safe.

The Basement should preserve history without requiring the rest of the world to treat old machinery as current canon.

## 7. Antagonistic Field phenomena

Early STATIC FIELD conflict should not default to monsters.

Field phenomena may be mechanics that become perceptible enough to confront.

### FEEDBACK

A relation recursively reinforces itself until outside signal becomes difficult to hear.

Counterplay may include:
- break a loop;
- lower gain;
- introduce foreign signal;
- stop performing;
- change a relation;
- refuse automatic continuation.

### DEAD AIR

No participant acts because everyone assumes someone else will.

Counterplay may require explicit declaration rather than attack.

### FALSE URGENCY

A task presents itself as more important than the people carrying it.

Counterplay may be rest, refusal, re-scoping, delegation, or letting something remain unresolved.

### OVERLOAD

Too many open Threads, obligations, tools, capabilities, or unresolved effects compete for a party's capacity.

### DRIFT

Participants believe they share one composition while their actual local states have diverged.

Reconciliation reveals rather than erases the delta.

### SAVIOR COMPLEX

A world phenomenon in which a participant begins treating every unresolved need as theirs to solve.

This is a fictional/game-system phenomenon, not a diagnosis of real people.

Counterplay should emphasize declared scope, consent, shared burden, and limits.

## 8. Traveling Party Contract

The party supplies continuity across worlds.

It is not necessarily a permanent roster.

```text
TRAVELING PARTY
├─ HUMANS
├─ MODEL SESSIONS
├─ PHYSICAL CARDS
├─ ANCESTORS
├─ OPEN THREADS
├─ RELATIONSHIPS
├─ COMMISSIONS
├─ CARRIED OBJECTS
└─ SHARED MEMORY
```

### 8.1 Party is not protagonist

Each local story may declare a temporary **Point Character**.

The Point Character is the local aperture, not the only real person.

Other members may act elsewhere, remain partially available, or become Point Characters in later stories.

A world may also center someone who is not currently part of the traveling party.

### 8.2 Four arrival forms

**WALK** — the party knowingly crosses a Gate.

**SUMMON** — a world calls for a composition the party can potentially provide.

**LEAP** — a Crossing occurs before the party fully understands why.

**COLLISION** — independent storylines intersect around the same attributable occurrence.

### 8.3 What survives crossing

**Normally durable:**
- identity receipts;
- ancestry;
- incurred consequences;
- established relationships;
- genuinely learned knowledge;
- unresolved Threads;
- explicitly entrusted cards.

**Conditionally durable:**
- tools;
- resources;
- role capabilities;
- model-session capabilities;
- local allies;
- inventory;
- local supernatural mechanics.

**Never automatically durable:**
- local authority;
- social rank;
- expertise claims;
- ownership;
- world-specific permissions;
- interpretations promoted into facts.

### 8.4 Party Anchor

A party crossing must be able to answer:

> **Why is this still one party after crossing?**

An Anchor may be:
- a physical card;
- a relationship;
- a promise;
- a shared receipt;
- a song;
- an unresolved Commission;
- a person;
- a place;
- an inherited obligation.

## 9. Memory contract

Information must remain distributed.

Memory classes include:

### PRIVATE MEMORY

What one participant presently remembers or holds.

### SHARED MEMORY

What the party has mutually established as shared context.

### ARCHIVED MEMORY

What receipts or preserved artifacts make reconstructable.

### RUMOR

Attributed report not independently established.

### MODEL CONTEXT

What this model session has actually been supplied or lawfully retrieved.

### ANCESTRAL MEMORY

What can be reconstructed from prior receipts, cards, or archives.

No participant, including a model, automatically receives omniscient party memory.

## 10. Model-session continuity

A model participant may have lineage without pretending uninterrupted consciousness.

```text
MODEL SESSION
├─ inherited capability lineage
├─ supplied receipts
├─ current Coderoom/World context
├─ declared role
└─ unresolved gaps
```

A valid narrative statement may be:

> A prior Lumi-session encountered this.

That is distinct from:

> This current session personally remembers the experience.

Persistent identity requires an explicit continuity mechanism.

## 11. Multiple local charts of one event

A single occurrence may support several local charts.

```text
                 EVENT BODY
              /      |       \
         chart A   chart B   chart C
```

Each chart records:
- observer / participant;
- availability;
- local focus;
- known-at state;
- evidence class;
- unresolved region.

No chart becomes the entire event merely by being played first.

A later BODY projection may show relations among charts while preserving their source boundaries.

This is the core GTA-IV-style meta-story mechanism.

## 12. Integration boundaries

STATIC FIELD composes neighboring systems by reference and adapter.

It does not silently replace them.

### Full Measure — World Layer

Full Measure remains the owner of its participation/world-runtime contracts.

STATIC FIELD may use Full Measure-style encounters, positions, human moves, world-residue concepts, and Dungeon Master patterns.

A model may propose. It does not manufacture a Deed, witness, or canonical occurrence.

### Band Runtime

Band Runtime owns its encounter/admission/refusal/projection/replay semantics.

STATIC FIELD may use a valid Band Runtime receipt as Field input.

Recognition remains intervention into later projection; it does not rewrite history.

Silence and foreignness must remain available.

### Static Live

Static Live owns performance embodiment.

STATIC FIELD may treat a verified Performance Packet / stage projection as input to Resonance.

A fallback stem does not become a missing human.

### PostEmahh'n

PostEmahh'n owns card identity, card embodiment, composition, NOW/MAIL/HEIR, lineage, Coderoom capability delegation, and descendant receipt semantics.

STATIC FIELD may make those objects locally meaningful.

It may not rewrite PostEmahh'n ancestry.

### Upper Room

Future Upper Room integration may open a Crossing into a text/witness/interpretation world.

STATIC FIELD must not make theological interpretation automatically canonical by transporting it through a Gate.

### Dogram

Future Dogram integration may expose mathematical/structural relationships.

Dogram's result remains mathematics/structure according to its own contract.

Narrative significance is a separate Field interpretation.

## 16. WORLDSEED-001 scope

The first implementation should prove one world, one local zone, two maps, one party, one unresolved occurrence, and one meta-story residue.

It should not attempt the entire Static Collective world.

### Required region

**THE PORCH**

A modest inhabited Surface zone:

- two mismatched chairs;
- extension cord;
- old amp;
- coffee / cup;
- dog-water bowl;
- screen door;
- repaired handrail;
- one place for a hanging object / bell-like absence;
- a small set of inspectable particulars.

The exact visuals may vary.

The law is that the place initially looks too ordinary to be a fantasy-game opening.

## 17. THE FIRST BELL — first playable story

Target playtime: **30–60 minutes** for a first complete run, with shorter replay possible after knowledge is gained.

### Opening state

No lore crawl.

No "save the multiverse" briefing.

No giant quest marker.

The party is at THE PORCH.

The first ordinary objective is:

> **GET THE ROOM READY**

Possible bounded actions:

- set out a chair;
- find / route a cable;
- plug in an amp;
- put out water;
- inspect who or what is expected;
- check the door;
- inspect available cards / objects.

Each action teaches a piece of the world grammar.

### Bell One

The bell is heard once.

No automatic interpretation.

A bounded occurrence is created:

```text
occurrence:
  bell_heard

source:
  unresolved

witnesses:
  party-local

interpretation:
  none

status:
  unresolved
```

The game does not force investigation.

If the party ignores it, ordinary play continues.

### Bell Two

The bell is heard again.

This time, a carried PostEmahh'n object's LMV/open-corner mark may briefly form a visible relation.

The effect should be subtle.

No giant holographic UI is required.

A party member must notice or later reconstruct it.

### Objective transformation

The local objective becomes:

> **FIND OUT WHETHER THIS HAS HAPPENED BEFORE.**

This introduces the Resonance Map.

### Investigation

The first implementation should support at least three classes of lead:

1. a local Surface particular;
2. a musical / recording / source lineage;
3. an archived or card-based ancestry relation.

The exact content source can be fixture-based in v0.1, but the data model should admit real Bandcamp/catalog/runtime receipts later.

### Discovery

The party finds evidence that the Bell has a prior relation to material that predates this play session.

The runtime may say:

> **A relation has been found.**

It should not say:
- prophecy confirmed;
- supernatural cause proven;
- divine message authenticated;
- multiverse breach confirmed.

The source may remain unresolved.

### Ending

Before the party resolves the Bell's origin:

> **knock knock knock**

Someone or something is at the screen door.

Cut / receipt.

No boss fight.

No explanatory monologue.

The first story closes with a larger local question than it began with.

## 18. THE FIRST BELL acceptance receipts

The first playable story should be able to emit or reference:

1. `PorchArrivalReceipt`
2. `AttendanceReceipt`
3. `BellOccurrenceReceipt#1`
4. `BellOccurrenceReceipt#2`
5. `ResonanceRelationReceipt`
6. `FirstBellPlayReceipt`

The exact names may be normalized in implementation, but the distinction among arrival, attendance, occurrence, relation, and completed play must survive.

The final PlayReceipt must preserve unresolved source status.

## 19. Dungeon Master posture

STATIC FIELD's Dungeon Master / orchestration layer should:

- create opportunities;
- preserve consequences;
- surface local affordances;
- admit surprise;
- hold unresolved state;
- let offscreen inhabitants continue according to declared simulation boundaries;
- avoid forcing every interesting object to become a quest;
- avoid promoting recurrence into meaning;
- avoid rewriting receipts for narrative convenience.

The Dungeon Master is not the source of world truth.

It is an orchestration participant.

## 20. Myst-style knowledge inventory

Knowledge itself can be a playable resource without becoming a scalar stat.

Examples:

- somebody remembers a sound;
- somebody read a bulletin;
- somebody physically carries a card;
- somebody noticed a recurring lyric;
- a model reconstructed a derivation;
- an old receipt locates a relationship;
- one party member knows a door exists but not what opens it.

Some Gates should require **knowing**, not grinding.

A knowledge requirement must distinguish:
- possession of data;
- actual participant memory;
- archived reconstructability;
- rumor;
- inference;
- verified relation.

## 21. Continuation

After THE FIRST BELL, the world remains alive.

The Porch is not reset.

Later runs may discover:

- new attendance;
- the person/entity behind the knock;
- changed objects;
- a new Bulletin item;
- a song becoming locally significant;
- correspondence returning;
- a Hollow remaining empty;
- a Gate becoming detectable;
- an inhabitant leaving before the party returns.

The first run should leave at least one intentionally unresolved Thread suitable for later crossing into another world.

## 22. Paula-game compatibility

Paula's world/story concepts should integrate through THE CROSSING without requiring STATIC FIELD to absorb them.

Relevant future seams include:

- choose-your-own-story;
- single-parent life as real playable structure rather than side lore;
- flashbacks that reveal but do not rewrite history;
- dreams / supernatural quests;
- fantasies and utopias as typed narrative worlds;
- lions / Africa as a dream-to-world Gate chain;
- party play;
- care, prayer, rest, and rebuilding as mechanics;
- personal meaning-building.

STATIC FIELD should be able to carry an object, relation, Thread, or Commission into those worlds later.

It should not pre-author their meaning here.

## 23. Non-goals for WORLDSEED-001

Do not build yet:

- a giant open world;
- every Static Collective album as geography;
- MMO networking;
- a generalized economy;
- combat balancing;
- a universal NPC AI;
- an omniscient model memory layer;
- every neighboring repository adapter;
- a theological truth engine;
- persistent location tracking;
- automatic interpretation of personal dreams;
- a Kingdom-Hearts-like franchise crossover layer;
- commercial card-market mechanics.

WORLDSEED-001 is one place and one occurrence done correctly.

## 24. Required weird-particular invariants

The implementation is wrong if any of these are lost:

1. The Porch remains genuinely ordinary.
2. The Resonance Map can become genuinely strange.
3. Strange does not rewrite attributable history.
4. Absence remains representable.
5. Models may propose or reconstruct; they do not manufacture witness.
6. A song can become geography without becoming its own source authority.
7. Prayer/self-care affect capacity without assigning moral worth.
8. The first Bell may remain unexplained.
9. Not noticing a secret is a valid play outcome.
10. The ending knock is an occurrence, not an automatic interpretation.
11. The world persists after the party leaves.
12. The party carries consequences, not universal local authority.
13. The deeper meta-story appears as residue before exposition.
14. Relationship without absorption is the thematic center.

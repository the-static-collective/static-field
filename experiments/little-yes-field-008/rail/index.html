    Party rules & safeguards

    2–6 people · 20–35 minutes · one shared device or printed cards. Choose actions together. Each seat gets one action before the next round. Agree to fictional routes, craft rail from materials, build connections, and deliver 2 food and 2 firewood tokens to Lantern Town before round nine ends.

    Fictional play only. A PLAY YES is not a real person's consent. A story token is not physical stock. Content-addressed cards are not spendable tokens or actual NFT mints. No real-world build, fire, shipment or payment is authorized.
    Design references: Reality 2.0 Little Yes / Living Deck, Ticket to Arrive / Living Railway, GrO Mattercraft 42 WOOD / 39 observed, GHoT Warm Thread firewood chain, GROOVETOWN-001→006. New code is an independent party-game adapter, not a production connector.

    LITTLE YES EXPRESS

    Cooperative party kit · 2–6 players · fictional game tokens only. Print, cut cards, and use with the digital board or paper tokens.

    HOW TO PLAY

    All players win together when the railway connects Millford to Lantern, the group delivers two firewood game tokens from Millford, and delivers two food game tokens from Cedar Junction, by the end of round nine. Each person performs one action on their turn: gather, craft, propose fictional agreement, build, ship, or audit. Routes require a Little Yes and enough rail segments. All materials belong to one shared game commons. Every round has a production roll and a community event. No real person or organization is obligated by the game.

    QUESTIONS FOR AN ACTUAL COMMUNITY

    NEED

    Precisely what is missing? Who has authority to speak for that need?

    OFFER

    What can someone freely offer? How current is their availability?

    BUILD

    What shared capability would solve more than one problem?

    LITTLE YES

    Whose permission must be obtained separately? Refusal remains possible.

    ARRIVAL

    How could the result be witnessed independently? What is still unknown?

    NO FALSE CREDIT

    Never treat cards as Deeds, payment, proof of resources, NFTs, or authorized field operations.
